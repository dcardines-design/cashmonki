# What's New — 3.7.8 (20)

Paste into App Store Connect → 3.7.8 → What's New in This Version.

---

Ask does more

• Reorganize categories in one go. Ask can add many categories and subcategories at once and move your existing transactions into them, all with one tap.
• Move everything from a merchant at once, like "Move all Grab rides to Transport".
• Find subscriptions you haven't tracked yet. Ask spots repeat charges and lets you add them in one go.
• Export your transactions as a CSV file.
• Create and switch wallets by chat.
• Talk instead of typing. Tap the mic in Ask.
• Tap a photo in the chat to see it full size.

Fixes

• Ask's totals now match your Transactions page.
• Questions from Ask come with buttons you can tap to answer.
