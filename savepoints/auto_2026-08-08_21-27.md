## Simulator Firestore crash — resolved, not an app bug
`simctl install` over the running app truncated Firestore's LevelDB WAL (`000269.log`), so
`FirestoreClient::Initialize` hit `FailAssertion` and SIGABRT'd on `com.google.firebase.firestore`
at launch. Fixed by deleting ONLY
`<container>/Library/Application Support/firestore` (6.4 MB) in container
`F6C5E44F-2B87-434F-AA58-738B814BEE29`; `Documents/ask_chat_*.json` and UserDefaults kept, chat
history survived. App relaunched clean. Note for later: Firestore offers no catch for this — the
hardening move would be `clearPersistence()` behind a repeat-crash flag. Low priority.
