# What's New — 3.7.6 (18)

Paste into App Store Connect → 3.7.6 → What's New in This Version.

---

A faster Ask

• Ask answers quicker. Adding a transaction usually takes about half as long as before.
• See what Ask is doing while you wait, like "Reading your photo…" or "Checking your budgets…".
• Keep typing while Ask replies. Send up to 3 follow-ups and they go out in order, or tap × to cancel one.

Fixes

• Tapping Add twice on an Ask card no longer adds the transaction twice.
• Ask's budget numbers now match your Budgets page, including spending in subcategories.
• Recurring items in another currency are converted before they're added to your spending totals.
• "Today" and "yesterday" in Ask follow your time zone all day.
